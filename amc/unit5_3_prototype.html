AI second brain for students in Germany

Your semester, connected.

Semesterhirn is the private AI second brain that turns your lectures, slides and notes into one exam-ready knowledge base, organised by course and linked to its sources.

Join the beta waitlist

Free during the beta. Planned launch for selected universities in summer semester 2027.

Built for how a semester actually works

01

One brain for every course

Stop hunting through LMS folders, PDFs and notebooks. Semesterhirn organises everything by course, lecture and concept, and connects ideas across lectures and semesters, so your knowledge builds up instead of resetting each term.

02

Exam-ready, not just organised

Set your exam dates, and Semesterhirn builds a review plan from your own material, with questions that focus on what you haven't reviewed yet. Built for the weeks when it matters most.

03

Every answer shows its source

Ask questions about your material and get answers that link back to the exact slide or page. You can check every answer, cite properly and use AI the way your university expects.

04

Private by design

Our commitments for launch:

  • Processing in the EU
  • No training of AI models on your content
  • No public sharing of your lecturers' material
  • Full export to Markdown
