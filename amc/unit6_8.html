Ancient Wuxia: The Flying Sword

A 10-second, 16:9 cinematic clip with sound, generated by my Cue personal AI agent from a single structured prompt.

🎬 The prompt (structured)

  • Subject & scene: a swordsman in traditional ancient Chinese clothing rides a flying sword across a sea of clouds and mountains.
  • Opening shot: cloud-shrouded peaks; he flies in from the distance, robes and long hair blowing in the wind.
  • Main action: he glides past valleys, waterfalls and cloud layers while the sword glows and the mist parts.
  • Visual style: realistic cinematic look, golden sunset, blue-green mountains, white sea of clouds.
  • Exclusions: no modern buildings, no aircraft, no extra characters, no text or subtitles.
  • Audio: howling wind, rustling robes and the whoosh of the sword; no dialogue, no music.
