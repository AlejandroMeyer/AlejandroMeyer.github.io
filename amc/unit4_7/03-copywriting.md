### Key Benefits

#### 1. It speaks Mittelstand finance
Zahlwerk is built for German controlling, not adapted to it after the fact. It works with DATEV exports, the SKR03 and SKR04 charts of accounts and leading mid-market ERPs. You can ask it questions in your own terms, such as *Deckungsbeitrag*, *BWA* or *Kostenstelle*, and it answers in German.

The AI agent prepares the data, builds your monthly close reports and answers follow-up questions, like a controller who never runs out of time. Your team works on the analysis, not on copy-and-paste.

#### 2. Works council ready from day one
In Germany, introducing AI is often slowed down by the works council, not by the technology. Zahlwerk ships with what those talks need:
- A **template works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung)** drafted with employment-law specialists, plus a technical fact sheet for the works council
- **Employee-level data anonymised or aggregated by default**, so there are no per-employee views unless you choose to turn them on
- A **data processing agreement (AVV)**, a list of subprocessors, a disclosure of how AI requests are routed, and a firm commitment that your data is never used for training
- AI-literacy onboarding material and transparency labels to support your EU AI Act obligations

Zahlwerk is a finance and controlling tool. It does not evaluate employee performance.

#### 3. One price, no surprises
You pay one flat monthly price per company, with AI usage included. There are no capacity units, no per-user AI surcharges and no bill that grows every time someone asks a question. A fixed-scope starter package lets you begin small and expand once the value is proven.

#### 4. Up and running in weeks, with your systems as they are
Zahlwerk works with the ERP you run today, so there is no S/4HANA migration and no data warehouse project first. Your controllers can keep Excel: it remains available as both an input and an export channel, alongside Power BI export.

**And every figure has a receipt.** Every number in a report or answer links back to the ERP or DATEV booking it came from, so your controllers and auditors can check it.

## Pitch Deck Key Points

*Ordered by Problem → Solution → Differentiation → Benefit. Statistics come from the research report; source tags refer to `01-research.md`.*

1. **Problem: the monthly close still runs on Excel.** Controlling teams in the Mittelstand spend days each month bringing together ERP, CRM and spreadsheet data by hand. [S19]

2. **Problem: today's AI BI is not built for them.** The main barriers are missing know-how (85% of non-users) and concerns about data protection (66%) and legal uncertainty (56%). Enterprise tools require DAX skills, paid capacity or an S/4HANA migration first. [S1][S12][S13]

3. **Solution: an AI controller, not another dashboard.** Zahlwerk's agent connects to the existing ERP, DATEV and Excel files, prepares the data itself and delivers German-language finance reporting, without a data team.

4. **Differentiation: finance depth.** Zahlwerk is built for German controlling: DATEV, SKR03/SKR04 and mid-market ERP logic, with German controlling terminology as the interface.

5. **Differentiation: works council readiness built into the product.** A template works agreement, anonymisation by default and a complete GDPR and AVV package turn the biggest adoption barrier into a reason to buy.

6. **Differentiation: predictable and sovereign.** One flat price per company with AI included, hosted on German infrastructure with EU-only AI routing. 69% of firms would use a German AI provider, compared with 41% for a US one. [S10]

7. **Benefit: value in weeks, not quarters.** A fixed-price pilot automates the monthly close report on the customer's own data, with no migration and Excel kept as a channel.

8. **Benefit: figures the CFO can trust and defend.** Every number traces back to its source booking, giving controllers and auditors a full audit trail.
